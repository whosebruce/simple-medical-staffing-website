Help

Help with the SMWS App

Call us at +1 (760) 536-2178 or email info@simplemedicalstaffing.com.

Signing In

The app signs you in with your email, no password. Tap I Already Have an Account, enter your email and tap Email Me a Sign-In Code. Type the 6-digit code from the email into the app. A code works once and stops working after 10 minutes; tap Send a New Code for another.

No email? Check your spam folder, make sure it's the address you applied with, or call us.

New Here

Tap I'm New Here to apply. It takes about 3 minutes, and your answers are saved so you can finish on any device. Our team reviews every application and contacts you about next steps.

Shifts, Hours and Pay

Once you're set up to work, Shifts shows open shifts and their pay, Schedule shows what you're booked for, and To Do shows anything you still need before you can work. If something looks wrong with a shift, your hours or your pay, call or email us.

Deleting Your Account

Sign in, open Me, then tap Delete My Account and Ask to Delete My Account. We delete your account within 30 days. You can take the request back in the same place until then. If you can't sign in, email info@simplemedicalstaffing.com from the address on your account and we'll do it for you.

Some records, such as shifts worked, hours and pay, are kept as long as the law requires. Our Privacy Policy explains what is kept and why.

Your Information

Read our Privacy Policy. Please don't send patient information, Social Security numbers or license images by ordinary email; upload documents in the app.
