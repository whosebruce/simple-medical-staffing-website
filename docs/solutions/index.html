What we offer

Three services, one way of working.

Contract staffing

Longer-term placements for sustained coverage — professionals who join your team, learn your unit, and stay through the assignment.

Best for: seasonal census, leaves of absence, extended vacancies

Per-diem staffing

Shift-by-shift coverage: you tell us the gap, we look for the match, you confirm it.

Best for: call-outs, census spikes, weekend and night gaps

Direct placement

Permanent hires selected for fit with your team — not just a resume that matches the requisition.

Best for: permanent roles, hard-to-fill specialties, leadership

How it works

From request to first shift.

  1. 1

    Tell us the need

    Unit, shift, specialty, start date. Send what you have and we'll ask about the rest.

  2. 2

    We match for fit

    We look for a professional whose experience and availability fit what you described.

  3. 3

    You confirm

    Review the match, ask whatever you want to ask, and approve when you're ready.

  4. 4

    After the start date

    If something isn't working, tell us. We'd rather fix a placement than defend one.

The difference

A partner, not a portal.

Directors of nursing and administrators answer for coverage and quality. We want working with us to make that job easier instead of adding to it.
