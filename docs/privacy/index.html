Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026. Covers this website and the SMWS app.

Who we are

Simple Medical Workforce Solutions (“we”, “us”) is a healthcare staffing agency in Southern California, operated by True Spirit, LLC. This policy covers this website and the SMWS app: the iPhone app and the web app at app.simplemedicalstaffing.com, where healthcare professionals apply, sign in, pick up shifts and keep their records, and where our office staff run scheduling and hiring.

What we collect

On this website: nothing you type. The website has no forms, accounts or advertising trackers. Our website host may process basic technical information, such as an IP address, browser information and request logs, to deliver and protect the site.

In the SMWS app, only what you give us or what your work with us creates:

  • Account details: your name, email address and phone number, and an optional profile photo.
  • Your application: the role you want, city and state, work authorization and age answers, experience, shift preferences, license or certification type, number and expiration, and a professional reference.
  • Credential documents you upload, such as a license, certification or ID. Each file is checked for viruses before it is stored.
  • Your work with us: availability, shifts offered, booked and worked, clock-in and clock-out times, time cards, pay rates and earnings, training, HR requests, messages from the office, and notes and ratings about shifts.
  • Sign-in and device information: sign-in sessions, the devices you sign in on, passkeys or authenticator apps you set up, and, if you turn on notifications, your device's notification token.
  • Your choices, such as whether we may text you about open shifts and your notification settings.

The app does not collect your location, contacts or browsing activity, shows no ads, and contains no advertising or analytics trackers. “Get Directions” opens your maps app; it doesn't send your location to us.

How we use it

  • To review your application and check your credentials.
  • To offer, book and schedule shifts, record your time and pay you.
  • To send you sign-in codes and email about your application, shifts and account.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent fraud and fix problems.
  • To meet our legal, tax and healthcare staffing obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Healthcare facilities where you are offered or booked for a shift: your name, role, credential status and the shift details they need.
  • Service providers that run the app for us, under contracts that limit what they may do with it: DigitalOcean (hosting, database and backups, in the United States), Resend (sending our email) and Microsoft 365 (our company email). If we pay you through the app, Stripe handles payment and tax details directly.
  • Government agencies or others when the law requires it, or to protect people's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you use it. Records of your application, credentials, shifts, hours and pay are kept as long as employment, tax and healthcare staffing rules require, then deleted. Backups are kept for up to a few weeks.

Deleting your account and your other choices

Delete your account in the app: sign in, open Me, then Delete My Account. We delete your account within 30 days. We delete your sign-in, photo, settings and the messages we sent you, and remove your name and contact details from the account. We keep your work record (application, credentials, shifts, hours and pay) only as long as the rules above require.

You can also ask us to show you, correct or delete your information, or change your choices, by calling +1 (760) 536-2178 or emailing info@simplemedicalstaffing.com. We confirm it's you before we act, and we answer within 45 days. California residents have these rights under California law, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

How we protect it

Information travels over encrypted connections. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and work once, office staff confirm sign-in with a second step, and only staff whose job needs your information can see it. Please don't send patient information, medical records, Social Security numbers or license images by ordinary email; upload documents in the app instead.

Children

The app is for adults applying for and doing healthcare work. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes and questions

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with its date, and tell app users in the app or by email when the change matters. Questions: call +1 (760) 536-2178, email info@simplemedicalstaffing.com, or write to us at 3333 Michelson Drive, Suite 300, Irvine, CA 92612. Help with the app is on the Help page.
